=== CHAPTER: Deadlock ===

Definition:
A deadlock is a condition where two or more processes wait indefinitely for resources held by each other.

Causes:
1. Mutual Exclusion
2. Hold and Wait
3. No Preemption
4. Circular Wait

Prevention:
Deadlock prevention eliminates one of the Coffman conditions.

=== CHAPTER: Process Scheduling ===

Definition:
Process scheduling determines which process gets CPU execution.

Algorithms:
- FCFS
- SJF
- Round Robin
- Priority Scheduling
